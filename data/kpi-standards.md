# KPI references and local warning bands

All displayed values are synthetic. Colours are dashboard review priority, not clinical triage or validated Candex performance.

## Finnish references

- [STM: access to treatment](https://stm.fi/hoitotakuu): NONURGENT specialist assessment must START within 3 weeks (21 calendar days) from RECEIPT of the referral. This is an outer legal limit, not a cancer-specific recommended waiting time. Candex LOCAL warnings: green≤14 days, yellow15–21, red>21. A regional median does not prove individual compliance.
- [Finnish Cancer Strategy2026–2035,p5](https://stm.fi/documents/1271139/1329769/Kansallinen_syopastrategia_2026_2035.pdf/5057c3d1-e824-3933-0a32-7344843e9bf6/Kansallinen_syopastrategia_2026_2035.pdf?t=1762756763489): strategic reference80% screening participation in every wellbeing county and≥70% classifiable cancers diagnosed at stagesI–II. Target horizon2035. Candex LOCAL warnings: green at/above target, yellow up to10percentage points below, red larger gap. Maakunta geometry must not be substituted for official wellbeing county reporting.
- [THL Ohjaus21/2016,updated2025](https://www.julkari.fi/bitstream/handle/10024/131630/Hoitoonp%C3%A4%C3%A4syn%20seuranta%20sy%C3%B6v%C3%A4n%20hoidossa_p%C3%A4ivitys170225.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=6): cancer-specific pathways and event definitions are needed. Comparison bins are not recommended target times. Three/six calendar-month legal limits are not converted into90/180day cancer targets. Goal3 treatment timeliness remains unassessed without the applicable pathway.

## Recognised external comparators

[NHS England cancer waiting-times monitoring guidance](https://www.england.nhs.uk/long-read/national-cancer-waiting-times-monitoring-dataset-guidance/): operational references96% treated within31days of decision-to-treat/earliest clinically appropriate date, and85% first definitive treatment within62days of the applicable urgent referral. These are ENGLAND benchmarks, not Finnish standards or universal international rules. Candex LOCAL warnings are yellow within5percentage points below the reference and red beyond that. Cohorts and anchors must match before comparison.

## Measures without a universal applicable threshold

Time to first radiologist review, overall median time to diagnosis, AI sensitivity/false-positive rates, follow-up completion, regional gap, demographic performance gap and research completeness have no universal national or international target asserted here. They stay grey in the default profile. Optional Local Demo bands in kpis.js illustrate configurable operating targets; they are not clinical or national standards.

Sources checked14November2026. Missing or invalid values remain grey in every profile. Colour is always accompanied by text and a source/interpretation panel.
